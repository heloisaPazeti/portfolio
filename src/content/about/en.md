I am a Computer Engineering student at the University of São Paulo (USP), currently on an exchange program at Arts et Métiers in France. I enjoy exploring different areas of technology, particularly software development, embedded systems, and the interaction between hardware and software.

Throughout my journey, I have worked on projects ranging from web applications and APIs to embedded firmware, robotics, and game development. These experiences have taught me to navigate different layers of development, from low-level programming to building applications that people interact with directly.

I am naturally curious and enjoy learning by doing, experimenting with ideas, and tackling new challenges. I am always looking for opportunities to deepen my knowledge, collaborate on interesting projects, and build technology with purpose.
